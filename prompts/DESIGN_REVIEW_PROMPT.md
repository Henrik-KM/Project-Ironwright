# Design Review Prompt for Codex

Review the proposed change against `AGENTS.md` and `docs/DESIGN_LOCKS.md` before implementation.

Return:

1. the player-facing strategic decision created;
2. any recurring task introduced;
3. how workload changes between 10 and 100 robots;
4. whether the feature moves the game toward RTS, colony, factory, tower-defence, or survival-meter conventions;
5. how routine execution becomes autonomous;
6. whether the base remains one constrained Heartforge;
7. whether enemy behaviour remains organic and causal;
8. the smallest prototype that could validate the idea;
9. a verdict: **accept**, **redesign**, or **reject**.

Reject a feature that adds operational maintenance without a stronger, simpler strategic payoff.
